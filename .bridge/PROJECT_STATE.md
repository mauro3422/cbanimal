## Full-body walking correction

The earlier correction was incomplete: it increased shin rotation but did not prove a coherent pelvis-thigh-knee-foot chain or world-space support behavior. The current walk replaces that approach.

### Analytical leg chain

The walking action is now generated by `scripts/refine_proxy_v5_ik_walk.py` as a two-bone sagittal chain:

```text
pelvis → thigh → shin → foot
```

- Both thighs participate in the step; the walk no longer depends mainly on shin rotation.
- The sagittal solver fixes the knee bend direction, serving the same purpose as a forward pole vector and preventing lateral inversion.
- Hip-to-ankle distance is capped at 97% of total thigh-plus-shin length, preventing a fully straight or telescopic leg.
- Segment lengths remain constant throughout the cycle.
- The free foot follows a raised arc instead of a straight interpolation.
- The support foot has an explicit planted phase.

Authored cycle:

- Frames: `1–13` at 24 fps.
- Authored logical duration: `0.5 s`.
- Exported GLB clip duration: approximately `0.541667 s`.
- Stance fraction: `0.5`.
- Stride half-length: `0.65` Blender units.

Readable pose map:

| Phase | Left | Right |
|---|---:|---:|
| Contact | 1 | 7 |
| Compression | 2 | 8 |
| Passing | 4 | 10 |
| Elevation | 6 | 12 |

Measured leg motion:

- Thigh local angle range on each side: `-8.312°` to `57.306°`, a range of `65.618°`.
- World-space knee flexion on each side: `29.597°` to `99.389°`.
- Maximum extension ratio: `0.966835`, below the `0.97` limit.
- Support-foot minimum Z: exactly `0.005` on both sides.
- Swing-foot clearance reaches approximately `0.528` on the left and `0.523` on the right.

### Pelvis and upper body

- Pelvis lateral weight shift: `0.14` Blender units.
- Pelvis rise/fall: `0.092971` Blender units, about 5.5% of total leg length.
- Pelvis roll/yaw, spine, chest, head, arms, and tail counterbalance the step.
- The body now lowers during compression and rises through passing/elevation instead of translating as a rigid block.

### Runtime cadence

The exported GLB was calibrated separately from Blender frame timing:

- Exported walk reference speed: `1.385` game units/s.
- Player movement speed: `2.2` game units/s.
- Walking action time scale: approximately `1.58845`.
- Effective runtime cycle duration: approximately `0.3410 s`.

`CharacterModel.setWalkingSpeed()` derives animation cadence from movement speed instead of allowing both values to drift independently.

### World-space foot locking

`Player.applyWalkingFootLock()` anchors the current support foot in world XZ space:

1. Detect the active support leg from the preserved walking phase.
2. Capture its world position when support changes.
3. Offset the player root by the difference between the anchor and current foot position.
4. Limit correction to `0.15` units per frame.
5. Clear the anchor on idle, emotes, sitting, blocking, or collision rollback.

A three-second simulation with four abrupt 90° direction changes measured:

- Without foot lock: maximum planted-foot drift `0.212447`.
- With foot lock: maximum planted-foot drift `4.44e-16`.
- Maximum root correction in one frame: `0.044043`, below the `0.15` safety bound.
- 18 support-foot switches were exercised.

### Turning

- Direction input no longer snaps the character orientation instantly.
- Rotation uses quaternion slerp with a `0.16 s` response time.
- Translation follows the gradually rotated body direction.
- The current walking action and phase are preserved through turns; the legs are not reset to frame 1.
- Foot locking makes the body pivot around the support leg rather than dragging both feet sideways.

## Limitations and next milestone

- v5 remains a hybrid low-poly gameplay proxy rather than a final unified 8,000–12,000 triangle production mesh.
- Most limb, face, paw, ear, and tail components remain separate rigid pieces.
- Runtime foot locking is root compensation driven by the animated feet, not a fully procedural per-leg Three.js IK solver.
- UVs, a 1024 texture set, final production weights, richer controls, and close-up deformation polish remain incomplete.
- The next decision should be based on a fresh normal-gameplay recording of this corrected build, not further tuning against the previous broken cycle.

Update this file whenever the verified milestone changes.
